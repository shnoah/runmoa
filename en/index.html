FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What devices and workouts are supported?

RunMoa is an iPhone app optimized for running workouts saved to Apple Health by Apple Watch. Compatible workouts from other apps can also appear, although some details may be unavailable.

Does RunMoa upload health data?

No. Apple Health workouts, routes, heart rate samples, notes, and shoe data stay on device. Distributed builds use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics without attaching those sensitive details.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. You can also explore a bundled sample run before granting Apple Health access.

Are condition and race predictions medical advice?

No. They are training references only and are not intended for diagnosis or treatment.
