RunMoa Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

RunMoa reads running workouts from Apple Health and presents them in a runner-focused format on your iPhone.

Overview

No account is required. RunMoa does not include advertising or social tracking and does not upload Apple Health running data, routes, heart rate samples, notes, shoe data, selected photos, or finished images to a developer server.

Distributed TestFlight and App Store builds use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These services may collect identifiers, product interactions, crash reports, performance data, device and OS information, app version, and diagnostic metadata. RunMoa does not attach Apple Health details, routes, heart rate samples, notes, shoe data, photos, or user IDs to Firebase reports.

Health data we read

  • Running workouts and routes
  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • VO₂ Max
  • Walking/running distance and step count
  • Running speed and stride length
  • Workout effort on supported systems

This data is processed on device for summaries, condition estimates, heart rate zones, charts, records, shoe assignments, and sharing layouts.

Data stored on device

Settings, heart rate zone preferences, shoe information and assignments, run notes, personal record review results, and derived summary/chart caches are stored in the protected app container and excluded from automatic iCloud/Finder backup.

Photos selected for sticker editing

The iOS system photo picker provides only the photo you select. RunMoa resizes and combines it with a sticker on device. The selected photo and finished image are not uploaded. Add-only Photos permission is requested only when you save the finished image.

Data sharing

Data leaves the app only when you explicitly copy, save, or share a generated image or personal backup JSON. Backups exclude Apple Health originals and detailed samples.

Your controls

  • Revoke Apple Health access in iOS Settings.
  • Delete shoe data and clear derived caches in RunMoa Settings.
  • Export or import a personal backup.
  • Delete the app to remove app-local data.
