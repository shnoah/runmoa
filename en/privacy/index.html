RunMoa Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

RunMoa reads running workouts from Apple Health and presents them in a runner-focused format on your iPhone.

Overview

No account is required. RunMoa does not upload Apple Health running data, routes, heart rate samples, notes, shoes, selected photos, or finished images to a developer server.

Distributed builds use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for usage statistics and diagnostics and display Google AdMob banners on Run Detail and Sticker Editing screens.

Advertising and tracking

Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform may process IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostics to provide and measure ads and for analytics.

Where required, RunMoa presents Google's consent message and Apple's App Tracking Transparency request before requesting ads. If you allow personalized ads and tracking, an advertising identifier may be used to link activity across apps and websites. If you decline, Google may provide non-personalized or limited ads when permitted. Available choices can be changed from Ad Privacy Options in RunMoa Settings and tracking permission can be managed in iOS Settings.

RunMoa never includes Apple Health workouts, route coordinates, heart rate, pace, cadence, elevation, notes, shoes, or photos in ad requests or uses them for advertising or targeting. The Mobile Ads publisher first-party identifier is disabled.

Health data we read

  • Running workouts and routes
  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • VO₂ Max
  • Walking/running distance and step count
  • Running speed and stride length
  • Workout effort on supported systems

This data is processed on device for summaries, condition estimates, heart rate zones, charts, records, shoes, and sharing layouts.

Data stored on device

Settings, heart rate zone preferences, shoes and assignments, notes, record review results, and derived caches are stored in the protected app container and excluded from automatic iCloud/Finder backup.

Photos and exports

The system photo picker provides only the photo you select. Composition happens on device. Images and personal backup JSON files leave the app only when you explicitly copy, save, or share them.

Firebase

Firebase may process identifiers, product interactions, crash reports, performance data, device and OS information, app version, and diagnostics. RunMoa does not attach Apple Health details, routes, heart rate, notes, shoes, or photos to Firebase reports.

Your controls

  • Manage available ad privacy choices in RunMoa Settings.
  • Change tracking and Health permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Delete shoes and clear derived caches.
  • Export or import a personal backup.
  • Delete the app to remove app-local data.
