RunMoa Privacy Policy

Effective: November 8, 2026

RunMoa reads running workouts from Apple Health and presents them in a runner-focused format on your iPhone.

November 8, 2026 revision: for the optional Direct Garmin integration offered from RunMoa 1.3.0, updated the Garmin categories, purposes, retention, international processing, rights and operational diagnostics to reflect current operations, and added that user-initiated iCloud and file backups include Garmin runs.

October 29, 2026 revision: added or clarified operator contacts, optional iCloud backup, Garmin operating commitments and service-provider disclosures.

Overview

No account is required. RunMoa does not upload Apple Health running data, routes, heart rate samples, run notes, shoe data, selected photos, or finished images to a developer server. If you use the optional Direct Garmin integration, Garmin activities are delivered to your iPhone through the server described in the Garmin sections below.

Distributed builds use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for usage statistics and diagnostics. RunMoa also displays Google AdMob banner ads on Run Detail and Sticker Editing screens.

Advertising and tracking

Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform may process IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostics to provide and measure ads and for analytics.

Where required, RunMoa presents Google's consent message and Apple's App Tracking Transparency request before requesting ads. If you allow personalized ads and tracking, an advertising identifier may be used to link activity across apps and websites. If you decline, Google may provide non-personalized or limited ads when permitted. You can change available choices from More > Data Management and manage tracking permission in iOS Settings.

RunMoa never includes Apple Health workouts, route coordinates, heart rate, pace, cadence, elevation, notes, shoes, or photos in ad requests or uses them for advertising or targeting. The Google Mobile Ads publisher first-party identifier is disabled.

Health data we read

  • Running workouts and routes
  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • VO₂ Max
  • Walking/running distance and step count
  • Running speed and stride length
  • Workout effort on supported systems

This data is processed on device for summaries, condition estimates, heart rate zones, charts, personal records, shoe assignments, and sharing layouts, except for the Apple service request described below.

Apple Maps and Weather services

For an outdoor run with an Apple Health route, RunMoa may send the first valid route coordinate and the run start time to Apple Maps and Apple Weather to resolve a concise location name and historical hourly weather. It does not request current-location permission. Weather is omitted when the route, eligible history, or required Apple Weather attribution is unavailable.

Only the resulting display location and weather values are cached on the iPhone. The representative coordinate is not stored in this cache or sent to Firebase, Crashlytics, AdMob, or a developer server. Apple's handling of these service requests is governed by Apple's applicable privacy terms.

Data stored on device

Settings, heart rate zone preferences, shoe information and assignments, run notes, personal record review results, derived summary/chart caches, and up to 100 display-only location/weather cache entries are stored in the app container with file protection and excluded from automatic iCloud/Finder backup. Original route coordinates are not stored in the location/weather cache. Garmin records downloaded to the device are stored the same way and excluded from automatic backup.

User-initiated iCloud personal backup

When you choose Back Up to iCloud Now, RunMoa stores one current snapshot of shoe information and display order, run-to-shoe links, notes you wrote, monthly/weekly goal settings, and runs received through the Direct Garmin integration (original data including routes and heart rate) in your CloudKit private database. Restore this backup in More > Data Management > Backup & Restore, or delete it under Manage iCloud Backup on that screen. RunMoa does not perform automatic or continuous synchronization.

Run links and notes store an app-domain one-way opaque reference instead of the raw HealthKit workout UUID. Apple Health workout dates, distance, duration, routes, heart rate, pace, samples, sources, personal records, and derived analysis caches are excluded. If a workout with the same identifier is not yet on the device, its link or note remains pending locally; RunMoa does not guess a match from date, distance, or duration.

The backup is stored as an encrypted asset in the user's CloudKit private database and is not sent to a developer-operated server, Firebase, Crashlytics, Google Mobile Ads, or an advertising or analytics service.

Photos and exports

The iOS system photo picker provides only the photo you select. Photo and sticker composition happens on device. Add-only Photos permission is requested only when you save a finished image. Images and personal backup JSON files leave the app only when you explicitly copy, save, or share them.

detailMapCard hides the first and last 200 meters of its route by default. You can choose Full Route for the current editing session; that exported image may reveal the run's start and end locations as well as street or neighborhood names shown by Apple Maps.

Personal backups can contain shoe data and display order, run-to-shoe links, notes you wrote, monthly or weekly mileage-goal settings, and runs received through the Direct Garmin integration; they do not contain Apple Health originals or derived analysis caches.

Firebase

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may collect identifiers, product interactions, crash reports, performance data, device and OS information, app version, and diagnostic metadata. RunMoa does not attach Apple Health workout details, routes, heart rate samples, notes, shoes, location/weather values, or photos to Firebase reports.

Your controls

  • Revoke Apple Health access or tracking permission in iOS Settings.
  • Change available advertising consent choices in More > Data Management.
  • Reset shoes, notes, and goals or clear analysis caches in More > Data Management > Data Maintenance & Reset.
  • Export or import a personal backup file.
  • Create, restore, or delete your private iCloud personal backup.
  • Disconnect Garmin and keep or delete downloaded Garmin records in More > Data Management > Import Runs > Garmin.
  • Delete the app to remove app-local data. A separately created iCloud backup can be removed with the in-app delete action.

Flyover videos

Flyover creates a silent video of your route and metrics on your device and adds it to Photos only when you choose Save. Flyover videos show the full route, including its start and end. Displaying maps and creating videos loads the corresponding map areas from Apple Maps. Video rendering does not upload its inputs or finished video. The Garmin delivery described below is a separate server transfer; activity inputs are never sent to analytics, crash reporting or advertising services. Flyover preferences and temporary videos are excluded from personal-data backups.

Direct Garmin integration

Direct Garmin integration is optional. You can keep using all existing Apple Health features without it. There is no separate RunMoa sign-up: you authorize the connection in Garmin Connect, and the app uses an anonymous connection account (Firebase Authentication). RunMoa never receives your Garmin password. Before connecting, we ask separately for consent to health-information processing and to international processing.

Garmin data categories and purposes

Within the scope you authorize in Garmin Connect, we process your Garmin user identifier, activity identifier, type and name, start time, duration, distance, calories, pace and speed, heart rate, cadence, elevation, route, laps and supported sensor samples, recording device information and sharing permissions. Available items vary by device, activity and permissions. We also process the access and refresh tokens and connection status needed to keep the connection. For activities other than runs, we keep only a minimal receipt marker, not their contents.

The purposes are to let you view your runs and their details, calculate mileage and goal progress, prevent double-counting of the same workout, and create the share images and route replay videos you choose. We do not request your name, email address or daily wellness data for the Garmin integration.

Garmin data travels from Garmin through Google Firebase/Google Cloud servers controlled by RunMoa to your iPhone. The server stores activities temporarily in encrypted form. Apple Health originals, on-device analysis caches and existing shoe, note and goal backups are never uploaded to the Garmin delivery server.

Garmin retention and deletion

The server is used only for temporary delivery. Once the app confirms an activity is stored on the device, the server original, details and summary for that activity are deleted without undue delay. Activities the app has not downloaded are deleted no later than 30 days after first receipt. Duplicate notifications do not extend this period; a valid new correction has its own delivery period. If you do not open the app for more than 30 days, activities not yet downloaded can no longer be received.

Garmin connection identifiers, permissions and access and refresh tokens are kept only while needed to maintain the connection and are deleted when you disconnect. Activities downloaded to your device remain there until you delete them, independent of the server's 30-day limit. Garmin records downloaded to your device are stored only on this iPhone and are excluded from automatic iCloud/Finder device backups. When you run Back Up to iCloud Now or Save to File in Backup & Restore yourself, your Garmin records (original data including routes and heart rate) are included. The iCloud backup is stored as an encrypted file in the CloudKit private database of your Apple Account, which the operator cannot access, and you can delete it in the app's iCloud backup management. You choose where a backup file is stored and manage sharing and deleting it. Restoring adds only Garmin records that are not already on this iPhone (from 1.3.0).

The server is not a backup for restoring activity history, and we do not keep separate recovery backups or long-term copies of activity data. After reinstalling the app or moving to a new device you must reconnect Garmin. Runs received earlier can be restored from an iCloud personal backup or backup file you created; without a backup they cannot be restored. Garmin Starter connections do not provide access to historical data.

Minimal delivery receipt markers and technical logs are kept separately from activity contents. Routine operational logs are kept for 30 days. Activity payloads, routes, heart-rate samples, authorization codes and tokens are never logged. Administrator access records for the processing system are kept for two years for security purposes and are not used for marketing. Google's internal deletion and security audit records follow each service's procedures and are not activity recovery backups.

Garmin operational diagnostics

To troubleshoot connection and delivery problems, Google Cloud Logging processes connection stages, times, allowlisted error categories, connection state, app version and a temporary diagnostic reference, kept for 30 days. Google Cloud Monitoring receives only aggregate counts of connections, reservations, delivery queues and server usage; diagnostic references are not used in these metrics.

The app keeps up to 80 recent technical entries in a protected file and rotates the diagnostic reference on next use after 24 hours. You can share up to 20 entries only by choosing Copy on the Support screen; nothing is sent automatically. These entries are excluded from OS backups and personal backups. Activity contents, routes, heart rate, notes, shoes, photos and tokens are never included in operational diagnostics.

Connection-capacity management uses hashed account and installation references with connection generation and status. If a disconnection cannot be confirmed with Garmin, this minimal marker may remain after the connection record is deleted, and is removed once confirmed.

Garmin disconnection and your rights

You can disconnect Garmin in the app's Data Management. Disconnecting requests deregistration from Garmin, stops new deliveries, deletes server activity data, tokens and connection information, and requests deletion of the anonymous connection account. You can choose to keep or delete Garmin activities already on your device. Deleting removes Garmin originals, details, caches and the notes and shoe assignments for those activities; a minimal deletion setting may remain on the device to prevent the same activities from being received again. Apple Health originals, the shoes themselves, personal backups you already created and photos or videos you saved elsewhere are not deleted. You can also change sharing permissions or disconnect in Garmin Connect; once the server confirms this, it disconnects and deletes server data.

Deleting the app alone does not immediately delete the Garmin connection or data on the server. Please disconnect in the app before deleting it, or contact us below. Undelivered server copies are deleted after at most 30 days. Separately created iCloud personal backups and files you exported to Photos or other apps must be managed where they are stored.

To request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, withdrawal of consent, or a copy of available data, contact pnr.run.apps@gmail.com. We verify only the minimum information needed to confirm your identity and never ask you to email Garmin passwords, authorization tokens or entire workout files. Requests through legal guardians or authorized representatives are handled under the applicable procedures.

Corrections to Garmin originals may need to be made in Garmin Connect. RunMoa does not promise editing features the original provider lacks, and will explain what data it holds can be corrected or deleted and any limits. Requests not restricted by law are handled without undue delay, and we report the outcome.

Processors, external sharing and international transfers

Server authorization, delivery, temporary storage and security operations for the Garmin integration use Google Firebase and Google Cloud. This server processing is separate from Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics, to which activity data is never attached, and from the roles of Apple Maps and Weather, iCloud/CloudKit and Google analytics, diagnostics and advertising services.

Apple Health and Garmin activity data are never sold or used for ad targeting. Garmin activity payloads, routes, heart rate, account-link identifiers and tokens are never sent to advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or AI services. Garmin data and derived data are never used to train, evaluate or improve AI models. Images, videos or files you choose to share may include your activity information.

The activity delivery function and database are configured in Seoul, South Korea. The following processing takes place outside Korea, and we ask for consent to international processing before connecting:

  • Items: Firebase Authentication anonymous credentials, IP address and user agent
  • Country and recipient: United States, on infrastructure operated by Google LLC and its affiliates (contact: Google privacy help page)
  • Timing and method: over HTTPS from when you start a Garmin connection
  • Purpose: installation authentication and security
  • Retention: after a disconnection is processed, we request deletion of the anonymous account. Google states that clearing deleted authentication data from active and backup systems can take up to 180 days.
  • App Check and operational diagnostics use Google's global infrastructure and may be processed outside Seoul.
  • How to refuse and effect: if you refuse international processing, only the Garmin connection is unavailable; existing features are unaffected.

Health information consent and safeguards

Before connecting Garmin, we ask separately for consent to processing health information such as heart rate and for consent to international processing; both are off by default. Each consent explains the categories, purposes, retention and the effect of refusal, which you can also review in this policy. Refusing or withdrawing consent prevents use of the Garmin integration but does not affect existing Apple Health features. Advertising and tracking consent is never bundled with Garmin connection consent.

We apply per-user access control (app authentication and App Check), encryption in transit, encryption of stored data and tokens, a least-privilege service account, administrator access management, retention-based deletion and incident-response procedures. Direct Garmin integration is available only to people aged 14 or older.

Service providers and website

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: Processors for Garmin authorization, delivery, temporary storage and security operations. Google's published terms identify Google Cloud Korea LLC and Google Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd. and relevant affiliates for Cloud services with Korean billing addresses. The Garmin execution region is Seoul; Firebase Authentication is processed in the United States. See contracting entities, subprocessors and processing countries, and Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google AdMob / UMP: Process the usage, diagnostic and advertising information described above. Garmin activity data is not sent to these services. Under Google's policy, Crashlytics starts deleting crash reports and associated identifiers after 90 days. Analytics and advertising retention and deletion follow their applicable settings and policies; the Garmin activity 30-day limit does not apply to them. See Google privacy and contact information and Analytics retention.
  • Apple: Handles Apple Maps and Weather requests and the private iCloud/CloudKit backup you choose, within the request and backup scope described here. See Apple privacy and rights information.
  • Garmin: Provides the Garmin Connect account connection and source activities you authorize. Your Garmin account itself is governed by the Garmin Connect privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): Hosts this public website, logo and policy. IP addresses and request information may be processed when these resources are requested. Garmin activities and tokens are not stored here. The website stores language preferences in browser localStorage; clear site data in your browser to remove them. See GitHub privacy information.
  • Google Gmail: Processes the sender address and content of inquiries you send us. We use them to respond and handle rights requests, retain them for one year after resolution, and then delete them. Any legally required separate retention will be explained with its basis and period. Do not send passwords, authorization tokens or entire workout files.

Server requests can include technical information such as IP address, time, request URL, status code and user agent. Current foundation-server retention settings are 30 days for routine logs and 400 days for Google Cloud required audit logs. These are separate from the server retention of Garmin activities (at most 30 days). We do not represent all international infrastructure and support processing as occurring exclusively in Seoul.
