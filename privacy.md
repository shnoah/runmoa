# RunMoa Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

- English: <https://shnoah.github.io/runmoa/en/privacy/>
- 한국어: <https://shnoah.github.io/runmoa/ko/privacy/>

RunMoa processes Apple Health running data on the iPhone and does not upload workouts, routes, heart rate samples, run notes, shoe data, selected photos, or finished images to a developer server.

Distributed builds use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for usage statistics and diagnostics and display Google AdMob banners on Run Detail and Sticker Editing screens. AdMob may process approximate location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, and diagnostics. Where required, RunMoa requests consent and App Tracking Transparency permission before loading ads.

RunMoa never includes Apple Health workouts, routes, heart rate, pace, cadence, elevation, notes, shoes, or photos in ad requests or uses them for advertising or targeting.

For the full policy, use one of the localized links above.
