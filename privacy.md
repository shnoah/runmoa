# RunMoa Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

- English: <https://shnoah.github.io/runmoa/en/privacy/>
- 한국어: <https://shnoah.github.io/runmoa/ko/privacy/>

RunMoa processes Apple Health running data on the iPhone and does not upload workouts, routes, heart rate samples, run notes, shoe data, selected photos, or finished images to a developer server.

Distributed TestFlight and App Store builds use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for usage statistics and crash diagnostics. RunMoa does not attach Apple Health details, routes, heart rate samples, notes, shoe data, photos, or user IDs to Firebase reports.

For the full policy, use one of the localized links above.
