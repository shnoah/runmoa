FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which running workouts can I import?

RunMoa imports running workouts saved to Apple Health. Allow Health read access on your iPhone to view your workouts. Available details depend on the data saved to Apple Health.

Does RunMoa upload health data?

No. RunMoa does not upload Apple Health workouts, route coordinates, heart rate samples, notes, or shoe data to a developer server. For an outdoor run with a route, it may send the first valid route coordinate and run start time to Apple Maps for a concise location name and to Apple Weather through WeatherKit for historical weather. Distributed builds use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics without attaching those sensitive details.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. You can also explore a bundled sample run before granting Apple Health access.

Are condition and race predictions medical advice?

No. They are training references only and are not intended for diagnosis or treatment.
